# Demo video (30 seconds)

Space Apps rule: the demo is a video of **at most 30 seconds** (or at most 7 slides). A longer video makes the project ineligible for Global Judging. Video demos in English still benefit from English subtitles, and AI-generated audio must be acknowledged in descriptive text and in metadata.

Files:

- `demo/voiceover.txt`: the narration to paste into ElevenLabs. 71 words, about 27 seconds at a normal pace. Replace `[TEAM NAME]` first.
- `demo/subtitles.srt`: English subtitles with the same timing. Burn them in, or upload them with the video. Adjust the times to the real audio.

## Shot list

Record the screen while running the **Guided tour**, on the deployed site, in Firefox, at laptop width (1280 × 720 or 1920 × 1080). Then cut the recording to these shots. The tour steps are not shown in order: the video opens on a place, which is the clearest first image.

| Time | Narration | On screen | Recorded from |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | "We're team [TEAM NAME], from Barranquilla, Colombia." | Globe turning, Natural view, title in the header | Landing page |
| 0:02–0:05 | "Earth data change constantly. But is a change real, or just noise?" | Anomaly view, timeline dragged from 1880 to 2025 so the bands redden | Tour step 1 |

## Before recording

- Run the tour once first so the NASA responses are cached and nothing waits on camera.
- Read the numbers on screen; do not script them. They come from live data and can change when NASA publishes a new year.
- For 0:15–0:18 use whichever view actually shows opposite directions on the day. Do not narrate the Congo basin as a climate fact: its record shows a step around 2003 that may be an artefact of the reanalysis.

## Keep out of the video

- NASA logos, the "meatball", mission patches or flags. The rules forbid them in SI-assisted material, and the voice is SI-generated.
- Anything from the Pink Panther films: the character, the theme music, title art. The project shares only the name; the franchise's music and images are copyrighted, and the submission confirms that no copyrighted material is used without permission. Use royalty-free music or none.
- Recognizable people under 18, personal data, and commercial promotion.

## Hosting

Upload to YouTube (Unlisted is enough, Public is safer) or Google Drive with "Anyone with the link". Put this in the video description, which is the "descriptive text" the AI rule asks for:

> Pink Panther, NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. Screen recording of the live tool (https://pink-panther-pi.vercel.app/). Narration generated with ElevenLabs (AI voice) from a script written by the team. Application code written with Claude (Anthropic) under the team's direction. Data: NASA POWER (MERRA-2), NASA GISTEMP v4, NASA GIBS.

Also add "AI-generated voice: ElevenLabs" to the file's metadata (title or comment field in the editor's export settings).

Open the link in a private window to check it plays without signing in before pasting it into the form.
